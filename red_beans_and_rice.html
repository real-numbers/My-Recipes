Monday's Red Beans & Rice

A classic slow-simmered comfort dish

Part 1: Red Beans & Sausage

Ingredients

  • 1 pound Camellia beans (or Mexican beans / small red beans), brined 8–24 hours
  • 4 slices Bacon, rendered
  • 1 small Onion, chopped
  • 1 small Green pepper, seeded & chopped
  • 1 stalk Celery, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon Paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon Black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon Cayenne pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Thyme leaves
  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 3 cups Chicken broth
  • 6 cups Water
  • 8 ounces Andouille sausage, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 teaspoon Red wine vinegar

Instructions

  1. Render the bacon slices in a large pot until crispy. Remove the bacon (optional) and keep the fat in the pot.
  2. Sauté the chopped onion, green pepper, and celery in the bacon fat until soft. Add minced garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Stir in the paprika, black pepper, cayenne, thyme, and bay leaves.
  4. Pour in the brined beans, 3 cups of chicken broth, and 6 cups of water.
  5. Bring the mixture to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer covered for 45 minutes.
  6. Stir in the bite-size pieces of Andouille sausage and continue simmering until the beans are fully tender and creamy.
  7. Finish by stirring in 1 teaspoon of red wine vinegar just before serving.

Part 2: White Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Long grain white rice, rinsed until water runs clear
  • 1 tablespoon Butter
  • 3 cups Water
  • 1 teaspoon Salt

Instructions

  1. In a separate saucepan, melt 1 tablespoon of butter and sauté the rinsed rice for 1–2 minutes until lightly toasted.
  2. Add 3 cups of water and 1 teaspoon of salt. Bring to a boil.
  3. Once boiling, turn the heat down to low, cover, and simmer for 18 minutes.
  4. Turn off the heat and let the rice wait, covered, for 15 minutes to steam completely.
Serving Tip: Serve the rich, simmered red beans over a scoop of the steamed white rice and top with fresh scallions if desired!

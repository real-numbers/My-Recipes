3-Ingredient Pectin-Free Peach Sauce

  • 2 to 3 cups Peaches: Finely chopped or mashed (fresh, frozen, or canned all work).
  • ½ cup to ¾ cup Sugar: Adjust to your taste depending on how sweet your peaches are. Sugar acts as a natural thickener as it boils down.
  • 1 tablespoon Lemon Juice: Do not skip this. The acid in the lemon juice works with the natural pectin in the peaches to help the sauce set, balances the sweetness, and keeps the color bright.

Instructions

Bread Machine Method

  1. Load the Pan: Place the prepared peaches, sugar, and lemon juice directly into the bread machine baking pan.
  2. Select Setting: Lock the pan into the machine, close the lid, and select the Jam or Sauce cycle. Press Start.
  3. Blend (Optional): When the cycle finishes, carefully remove the hot pan with oven mitts. For a completely smooth, pourable sauce, blend it briefly with an immersion blender.
  4. Cool and Store: Let the sauce cool to room temperature, transfer it to a clean jar, and store it sealed in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks.

Traditional Stovetop Method

  1. Simmer: Combine all ingredients in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Bring to a boil, stirring constantly until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Thicken: Lower the heat and simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens to your liking.
  3. Cool and Store: Remove from heat, let cool, and transfer to a sealed container in the fridge.

Whole Wheat Bread Machine Mix

Ingredients

Flour Base Mix

  • 190g Whole Wheat Flour
  • 190g Bread Flour
  • 1 tbsp Vital Wheat Gluten
  • 1/4 cup Flax Seed Meal (~32g)
  • 1/4 cup Potato Flakes (~15g)

Packets & Mix-ins

  • Salt Packet: 3 tsp Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt
  • Yeast Packet: 1.5 tsp Bread Machine Yeast
  • Milk Powder Packet: 2 tbsp Dry Milk Powder
  • Mix-in: 1/2 cup Sunflower seeds

Whole Wheat Bread: What to add

  • Water: 1.25 cup Room temperature
  • Oil: 1.5 Tablespoons Olive oil or melted butter
  • Honey: 1.5 Tablespoons Honey or Molasses
  • Mix-in: 1/2 cup Sunflower seeds

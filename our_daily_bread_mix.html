Our Daily Bread Mix

Whole Wheat & Seed Bread Machine Mix (900g / 1.5 lb Size)

A 50/50 blend of whole wheat and bread flour with measured packets of milk powder, yeast and salt.

Ingredients

< <
Ingredient Amount Notes / Conversions
Warm Water 1 1/4 cups 265 ml (Approx. 80°F to 90°F)
Oil 2 tbsp
Honey 2 tbsp
Salt 1 ½ tsp
Whole Wheat Flour 1 ½ cups 180g
Bread Flour 1 ½ cups 195g
Dry Milk Powder 1 ½ tbsp
Instant Potato Flakes 2 ½ tbsp
Ground Flax 3 tbsp
Sunflower Seeds 1/2 cup Add manually at the machine's beep
Bread Machine Yeast 1 ½ tsp

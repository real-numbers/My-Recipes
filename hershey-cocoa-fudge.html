Hershey's Cocoa Box

Mary Kadel made this fudge whenever her chocolate cravings came to visit. She was a pro at identifying the soft ball stage without a thermometer.

Rich Cocoa Fudge

Ingredients

  • 3 cups sugar
  • 2/3 cup HERSHEY'S Cocoa
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1-1/2 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup (1/2 stick) butter or margarine
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Combine sugar, cocoa, and salt in a heavy 3-quart saucepan. Stir in milk.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until mixture comes to a full rolling boil.
  3. Boil without stirring to 234°F on a candy thermometer (or until syrup, when dropped into very cold water, forms a soft ball which flattens when removed from water).
  4. Remove from heat. Add butter and vanilla. Do not stir.
  5. Cool at room temperature to 110°F (lukewarm). Beat with a wooden spoon until fudge thickens and just begins to lose its gloss.
  6. Quickly spread into a greased 8- or 9-inch square pan. Cool completely; cut into squares. Yields about 36 squares (about 2 lbs.).

Editor's note: "The secret to smooth fudge is to never let an unmelted sugar crystal get into the hot pan of fudge while it's cooking, or it will start a chain reaction that will give you grainy, not creamy, fudge. Be careful when mixing ingredients not to splatter sugar up onto the sides of the pan. Then, once it starts bubbling, cover the pan for a minute to let the steam wash down the sides, just in case, and use a clean spoon each time you stir it."

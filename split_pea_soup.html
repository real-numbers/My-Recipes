Split Pea Soup with Ham

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins
🍳 Cook: ~4 hours
🍲 Type: Hearty Soup

Ingredients

The Base & Aromatics

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped

The Broth & Meat

  • 2 meaty ham shanks (not hocks)
  • 4 bay leaves
  • 3 quarts water

The Finish

  • 16 oz dried split peas
  • 1 tsp dried thyme

Instructions

  1. Saute the Aromatics: Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large soup pot. Saute the chopped onions, carrots, and celery until the onions just begin to brown, about 6 minutes.
  2. Simmer the Broth: Add the 2 meaty ham shanks, 4 bay leaves, and 3 quarts of water to the pot. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat. Cover partially and simmer for 2 to 2.5 hours until the ham is tender enough to easily pull away from the bone.
  3. Shred the Ham: Carefully remove the shanks from the pot. Allow them to cool slightly, then remove and discard the bones and cartilage. Shred the meat into bite-sized chunks and return them back to the soup pot.
  4. Cook the Peas: Stir the 16 oz of dried split peas and 1 teaspoon of dried thyme into the soup. Simmer uncovered for about 1 hour and 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the peas dissolve entirely and naturally thicken the soup.

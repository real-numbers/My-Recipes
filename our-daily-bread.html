750g Super-Charged Whole Wheat Loaf

Optimized for a 1.5 lb / 750g setting using your meal-prep assembly system. This recipe reins in the explosive rise while keeping your favorite ingredients.

1. The Liquids (Printed on the Lid)

  • Water: 1 cup + 2 tablespoons (270ml) *Adjusted up front for the flax/potato absorbency
  • Honey: 1.5 tablespoons
  • Oil: 1.5 tablespoons
  • Dry Milk Powder: 1.5 tablespoons

2. The 32 oz Container (The Flours & Structural Build)

  • Whole Wheat Flour: 190 grams
  • Bread Flour: 190 grams
  • Vital Wheat Gluten: 1 tablespoon
  • Flax Seed Meal: 1/4 cup (approx. 32g)
  • Potato Flakes: 1/4 cup (approx. 15g)

3. The Apothecary Packets (The Finishing Layer)

  • Salt Packet: 3 teaspoons Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt *Doubled to properly brake the yeast
  • Yeast Packet: 1.5 teaspoons Active Dry Yeast *Slightly governed to prevent lid-hits

4. The Mix-In Beep

  • Sunflower Seeds: 1/2 cup
Baking Instructions: Add ingredients to the machine pan in your standard order (liquids, then container flours, then top with your packets). Select the Whole Wheat cycle and the 750g / 1.5 lb setting.

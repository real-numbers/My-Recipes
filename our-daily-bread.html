750g Super-Charged Whole Wheat Loaf

Optimized for a 1.5 lb / 750g setting using your meal-prep assembly system. This recipe reins in the explosive rise while keeping your favorite ingredients.

Add ingredients to bread machine pan in the order shown below.

1. Put the liquids in the bread machine pan first

  • Water: 1 cup + 2 tablespoons (270ml) *Mix well with milk powder in a jar then add to pan.
  • Dry Milk Powder Packet 1.5 tablespoons
  • Oil: 1.5 tablespoons *Olive oil or melted/cooled butter
  • Honey: 1.5 tablespoons*Hint: Use the same spoon as used for the oil.

2. The flours (pre-mixed dry ingredients): *Add to pan on top of liquids and smooth the surface.

  • Whole Wheat Flour: 190 grams
  • Bread Flour: 190 grams
  • Vital Wheat Gluten: 1 tablespoon
  • Flax Seed Meal: 1/4 cup (approx. 32g)
  • Potato Flakes: 1/4 cup (approx. 15g)

3. The Salt and Yeast packets

  • Salt Packet: 3 teaspoons Diamond Crystal Kosher Salt *Put in the corners.
  • Yeast Packet: 1.5 teaspoons Bread Machine Yeast *Pour into a little crater in the middle of the flour

4. Put the pan in the bread machine and turn to tighten.

5. Plug in the machine.

6. Select #5, Whole Wheat, 750g (default), Color Med(default)

7. Press the large button, then press the small Start button.

8. Set your phone timer for 38 minutes.

  • Sunflower Seeds: 1/2 cup Add the sunflower seeds when the machine beeps in 38 minutes.

Southwestern Black Bean Salad

⏱️ Prep: 15 mins
🍳 Cook: 1 min

Ingredients

The Dressing

  • 2 tbsp ground cumin
  • 4 tbsp (¼ cup) lime juice
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt

The Mix-ins

  • 1 cup carrot, finely diced
  • 1 cup corn kernels, cooked
  • 6 tbsp or 1 bunch fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 4 tbsp (¼ cup) red onion, chopped
  • 3 cups black beans, cooked

Instructions

  1. Toast the Spice: Heat the 2 tablespoons of ground cumin in a small skillet over a low flame until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  2. Whisk the Dressing: In a mixing bowl, whisk together the toasted cumin, 4 tablespoons of lime juice, 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil, and ½ teaspoon of salt.
  3. Incorporate the Vegetables: Add the 1 cup of finely diced carrot, 1 cup of cooked corn kernels, 6 tablespoons of chopped cilantro, and 4 tablespoons of chopped red onion to the bowl. Mix everything together thoroughly.
  4. Fold in the Beans: Add the 3 cups of cooked black beans to the mixture. Use a rubber spatula to gently fold the beans in so they stay intact. Refrigerate overnight.

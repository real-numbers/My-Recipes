Bread Mix Instructions

  1. Take the pan out of the bread machine. Make sure the stirring paddle is there.
  2. Open the bread mix and remove the packets.
  3. Refer to the lid for the amounts of water, oil, sweetener (honey or molasses) and seeds that will be added to this mix.
  4. If there is a milk powder packet, shake its contents up in a jar with the amount of water specified on the lid, and pour the mixture into the pan, else just add plain water.
  5. Add the specified amounts of oil and sweetener to the water in the pan.
  6. Dump the bread mix into the pan and spread it out evenly on top of the liquids.
  7. Make a little crater in the middle for the yeast.
  8. Empty the salt packet into the corners and the yeast packet into the crater.
  9. Place the pan inside the machine and turn it clockwise to tighten.
  10. Plug in the bread machine.
  11. Turn the Select button to #5, for Whole-Wheat Bread, taking the defaults for weight (750g) and color (medium).
  12. Press the Select button, then press the Start button.
  13. Set your phone timer to 35 minutes if you will be adding seeds.
  14. After 35 minutes, open the lid and add the seeds.
  15. The machine beeps when the bread is ready. Put a rack on the cutting board on the counter and get a clean white kitchen towel to cover the bread as it cools.
  16. Unplug the machine. It's best to get the bread out right away, but the machine will keep it warm for 50 minutes if you don't.
  17. The machine, the pan, and the pan's handle will burn you if you let them!! Twist the pan counter-clockwise to release the latch, then use the little hook thingy to lift the handle. Grasp the handle with a pot holder and lift the pan out of the machine. Grab the bottom with a thick potholder.
  18. Invert and wiggle the pan to dump the bread out onto the rack. If the stirring paddle is not in the pan, it's in the bottom of the bread. Use the hook thingy and a pot holder to get ot out, because it's HOT!Put it in the pan, but not on the spindle.
  19. Put the pan & paddle back in the machine until cool. Then put them in the sink and add about an inch of water to soak. Clean with a dishcloth, not a scrubber.
  20. Cover the hot loaf with the towel, and let it cool for at least 30 minutes before slicing.

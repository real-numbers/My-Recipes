Whole Wheat Apple Crisp

AI Recipe
⏱️ Prep: 20 mins
🔥 Bake: 40-50 mins
🍽️ Yield: 6-8 servings

🛒 Ingredients

Apple Filling

  • 3 lbs tart apples (approx. 6–8 medium apples), peeled, cored, and sliced into 1/4-inch wedges
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar (or light brown sugar)
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose or whole wheat flour (to thicken the juices)
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (optional, skip if apples are already very sharp)

Whole Wheat Topping

  • 1 cup rolled oats (old-fashioned oats work best)
  • 1. cup whole wheat flour (adds a beautiful, rustic nuttiness)
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar (light or dark)
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, cold and cubed

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prep the Oven and Dish: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease the inside of oven-proof bakeware with a small pat of butter.
  2. Toss the Apples: In a large mixing bowl, gently toss sliced apples with the sugar, 1 tablespoon of flour, and 1 teaspoon of cinnamon until evenly coated. Arrange the slices flat and even in prepared bakeware.
  3. Pulse the Topping Base: In the bowl of food processor, combine the whole wheat flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, and cold, cubed butter. Pulse 5 to 8 times until the butter pieces are the size of small peas and the mixture resembles coarse sand.
  4. Incorporate the Oats: Add the rolled oats to the processor bowl. Pulse just 2 or 3 times to distribute them. You want the oats to stay mostly whole to retain that classic crunchy texture.
  5. Assemble: Pour the food processor crumble evenly over the apples in bakeware, scattering it completely to cover the fruit.
  6. Bake: Place on the center rack of fully preheated oven. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes, until the whole wheat topping is deeply golden-brown and the apple juices bubble lazily around the edges.
  7. Cool & Serve: Rest the dish safely for 10–15 minutes to let the juices set. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.

✨ Glass Bakeware Care Tips

  • Avoid Thermal Shock: Ensure the bowl is completely at room temperature before placing it into the oven. Never place a hot bowl onto a cold countertop, cold liquid, or a metal stove grate—rest it on a dry wooden cutting board or thick cloth potholders.
  • Preheat Fully: Never place glassware inside an oven while it is still preheating, as the direct cycle elements can stress older glass.
  • Hand Wash Only: Skip the dishwasher entirely to keep your glass bakeware's finish vibrant and shiny for generations to come.

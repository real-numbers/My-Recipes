Scaled-Back Whole Wheat & Seed Bread Machine Recipe (900g / 1.5 lb Size)

A perfectly balanced 50/50 blend of whole wheat and bread flour. The addition of instant potato flakes ensures a beautifully moist and tender crumb that stays soft for days, while ground flax and sunflower seeds offer a robust, nutty finish without overwhelming a 900g bread machine pan.

Ingredients

< <
Ingredient Amount Notes / Conversions
Warm Water 1 ⅛ cups 265 ml (Approx. 80°F to 90°F)
Butter or Vegetable Oil 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp (Softened or melted)
Honey or Brown Sugar 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp
Salt 1 ⅛ tsp 1 tsp + a generous pinch
Whole Wheat Flour 1 ½ cups 180g (Exactly half your flour base)
Bread Flour 1 ½ cups 195g (Ensures a strong, high rise)
Dry Milk Powder 1 ½ tbsp 1 tbsp + 1 ½ tsp
Instant Potato Flakes 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp (Unseasoned)
Ground Flax 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp (Add with dry ingredients)
Sunflower Seeds ¼ cup Add manually at the machine's beep
Bread Machine Yeast 1 ½ tsp Or instant yeast

Loading & Baking Instructions

  1. Add Liquids First: Pour the warm water, oil, and honey directly into your bread machine pan.
  2. Layer Dry Ingredients: Carefully add the salt, whole wheat flour, bread flour, dry milk powder, instant potato flakes, and your ground flax on top of the liquids.
  3. Create a Yeast Well: Dig a small, shallow indentation in the dry ingredients layer and add the yeast, making sure it stays dry and does not touch the liquid underneath.
  4. Select Machine Settings: Program your machine to the 1.5 lb / 750g–900g setting. Use either the Whole Wheat cycle (preferred for better flour absorption) or the Basic/White cycle. Choose Medium Crust.
  5. Add the Crunch: When your bread machine sounds its add-in alert (usually 15–20 minutes into the kneading process), drop the sunflower seeds straight into the dough.

💡 Baker's Notes

  • Why Ground Flax is in Step 2: Because ground flax acts like a heavy flour matrix rather than a standalone seed, incorporating it early allows it to distribute uniformly and stabilize the structural rise of the dough.
  • Dough Consistency Check: Open the lid 5 minutes into the kneading cycle. The mixture should form a smooth, tacky ball that clears the sides of the pan. If it looks too wet or sticky, sprinkle in 1 teaspoon of flour; if it looks dry or crumbly, drizzle in 1 teaspoon of warm water.

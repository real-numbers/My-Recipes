Mary Kadel's Lemon Meringue Pie

Baking: 325°F for 15-20 minutes

Process Overview

Blend the custard base and cook on medium heat until thickened, then carefully temper the egg yolks before combining. Stir in the lemon juice, rind, and butter, then pour into a pre-baked crust. Whip room-temperature egg whites with cream of tartar and sugar until stiff peaks form, spread immediately over the hot filling, and bake until beautifully golden. Ingredients

Custard:

  • 1 c. sugar
  • 1/4 tsp. salt
  • 1/4 c. flour
  • 2-3 T. cornstarch
  • 1 1/2 c. water
  • 2 egg yolks (save the whites)
  • 1 T. butter
  • 1/4 c. lemon juice
  • grated rind of lemon
  • Baked pie crust

Meringue:

  • 2 egg whites
  • 1/4 tsp cream of tartar
  • 4 T. sugar

Preparation Steps

Phase 1: The Custard

  1. Blend sugar, salt, flour, and cornstarch with water in a sauce pan.
  2. Cook on medium heat until thickened.
  3. Temper the egg yolks: Add some of the mixture to the beaten yolks, so as to warm but not cook the eggs.
  4. Combine and cook one minute, stirring constantly. Add lemon juice, rind, and butter. Stir and pour into the baked crust.

Phase 2: The Meringue

  1. Beat room-temperature egg whites on low-to-medium speed until soft, foamy bubbles form.
  2. Stabilize: Add the cream of tartar.
  3. Incorporate Sugar: Increase speed to medium-high and add the sugar gradually, 1 tablespoon at a time.
  4. Whip until thick, glossy, and holding stiff peaks.
  5. Spread over the hot pie filling immediately.

Phase 3: Baking

  1. Bake at 325 degrees for 15 - 20 minutes until the meringue peaks are starting to brown.

Our Daily Bread

Scaled for 900g (1.5 lb) Bread Machines | 50/50 Flour Blend
Ingredient Amount Notes
Warm Water 1 ⅛ cups 265ml (80°F - 90°F)
Butter or Oil 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp (softened/melted)
Honey or Brown Sugar 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp
Dry Milk Powder 1 ½ tbsp 1 tbsp + 1 ½ tsp (dissolve in liquids)
Salt 1 ⅛ tsp 1 tsp + a generous pinch
Whole Wheat Flour 1 ½ cups 180 grams
Bread Flour 1 ½ cups 195 grams
Instant Potato Flakes 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp (unseasoned)
Ground Flax 2 ¼ tbsp 2 tbsp + 1 tsp
Bread Machine Yeast 1 ½ tsp Instant yeast works too
Sunflower Seeds ¼ cup Hold back for the mix-in beep

Bread Machine Loading Order

  1. Liquids & Milk Powder: Pour the warm water, oil, and honey into the bread pan. Whisk or stir the dry milk powder directly into these liquids so it dissolves completely.
  2. Dry Base & Flax: Add the salt, whole wheat flour, bread flour, instant potato flakes, and ground flax directly over the liquid base.
  3. Yeast: Create a small, shallow well in the center of the dry ingredients and add the yeast, ensuring it stays dry and does not touch the liquid underneath.
  4. The Beep: Close the lid, select the 1.5 lb / 900g setting on a Whole Wheat or Basic cycle, and choose a Medium Crust. Add the sunflower seeds manually when your machine beeps for mix-ins.
Baker's Note: Ground flax acts like a flour and needs to be mixed from the start to develop proper gluten structure, while the dissolved milk powder and instant potato flakes guarantee an incredibly moist, tender crumb that won't go stale quickly.

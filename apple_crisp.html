Classic Apple Crisp

Scaled for 3 lbs of tart apples & baked in a family heirloom Pyrex bowl
⏱️ Prep: 20 mins
🔥 Bake: 40-50 mins
🍽️ Yield: 6-8 servings

Ingredients

The Apple Filling

  • 3 lbs tart apples (approx. 6–8 medium), peeled, cored, and sliced into 1/4-inch wedges
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar (or light brown sugar)
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (optional)

The Crisp Topping

  • 1 cup rolled oats (old-fashioned)
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, cold and cubed

Instructions

  1. Prep the Oven and Dish: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease the inside of your oven-safe vintage Pyrex bowl with butter or cooking spray.
  2. Mix the Filling: In a large bowl, toss the sliced apples with sugar, 1 tablespoon of flour, and 1 teaspoon of cinnamon until evenly coated. Transfer into your prepared Pyrex bowl, pressing them down slightly so they sit level.
  3. Make the Topping: In a separate medium bowl, combine oats, flour, brown sugar, remaining cinnamon, and salt. Add cold, cubed butter. Work the butter in using your fingers or a pastry cutter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs and clumps together when squeezed.
  4. Assemble: Scatter the oat topping evenly over the apples in your Pyrex bowl, covering them completely.
  5. Bake: Place the bowl on the center rack of your fully preheated oven. Bake for 40 to 50 minutes, until the topping is deeply golden-brown and the juices bubble around the edges.
  6. Cool & Serve: Let cool for 10–15 minutes to let the juices thicken. Serve warm, ideally with vanilla ice cream!

✨ Vintage Pyrex Care Tips

  • No Sudden Temperature Changes: Ensure the bowl is at room temperature before putting it into the preheated oven.
  • Safe Landing: Place the hot bowl onto a wooden cutting board or thick cloth potholder—never directly onto a cold countertop or wet surface.
  • Hand Wash Only: Protect the 1960s finish and color from dishwasher fading.

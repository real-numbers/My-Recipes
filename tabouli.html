Tabouli from Simply Vegetarian

⏱️ Prep: 20 mins
⏳ Soak/Chill: 2 hours
🥗 Total: ~2.5 hours

Ingredients

The Bulgur

  • 4 cups water
  • 2 cups fine dried bulgur

The Dressing

  • ½ cup lemon juice
  • 2–4 tbsp fresh mint, minced
  • 4 cloves garlic, pressed
  • 2 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ cup olive oil

The Vegetables

  • 1½ cups fresh parsley (approx. 2 large bunches), finely chopped
  • ¾ cup green onions (including tops), chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup cucumber, peeled and chopped

Instructions

  1. Prepare the Bulgur: Bring the 4 cups of water to a boil. Remove from heat, stir in the 2 cups of fine dried bulgur, cover, and let soak for 1 hour.
  2. Make the Dressing: Meanwhile, in a large mixing bowl, combine the lemon juice, minced mint, pressed garlic, salt, and black pepper. Slowly whisk in the olive oil until well incorporated.
  3. Toss the Vegetables: Stir the finely chopped parsley, green onions, chopped tomatoes, and chopped cucumber directly into the dressing.
  4. Combine and Chill: Mix the vegetable and dressing mixture into the warm, soaked bulgur. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour to allow the flavors to meld. Stir well right before serving.
